How to use this

Works out how much of each metal to combine for an alloy, and how to split it into crucible-sized batches if the total is more than your crucible can hold at once.

Setting up metals

  • Click + to add a metal. Click its colored square to change its color.
  • Type the metal's name, then set its allowed percentage range with the slider or the two boxes underneath.
  • Untick "Range" if the recipe calls for one exact percentage rather than a range.
  • Optionally enter how much of that metal you have. Leave it blank if you have (or can get) as much as you need.

Getting a result

If at least one metal has an amount entered, the calculator finds the largest possible batch and the exact ratio that uses your scarcest materials most efficiently. If no metal has an amount entered, it just gives you an average alloy within the ranges.

If the result is bigger than your crucible cap, it's split into multiple batches - each one still respecting every metal's percentage range on its own, not just on average.

Units & partial amounts

Pick whichever unit you track materials in (millibuckets, ingots, or nuggets) in Settings, along with your mod's conversion rate. Turn on "Allow partial amounts" if your setup lets you use fractional units - otherwise everything is rounded to whole units.
